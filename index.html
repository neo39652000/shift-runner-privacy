Privacy Policy – Shift Runner

Shift Runner for Android, by Little Order Studio

Last updated: 22 November 2026 (version 3.9.4)

Summary

Shift Runner is a running game. Your coins, gems, outfits, progress and settings are saved only on your phone. The only data that leaves your phone is for the online weekly leaderboard: an anonymous player ID, a random runner name made by the game, and your scores. We never ask for your real name, email, phone number or location, we do not show ads, and we never sell your data. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play.

Who we are

Shift Runner is developed by Little Order Studio (Mauro Todini, Australia) ("we", "us"). This policy applies only to the Shift Runner app.

Information stored on your phone

  • Your coins, gems, unlocked worlds, outfits, upgrades, best scores, settings and purchase status are stored only on your device.
  • We do not have a copy of this data. If you uninstall the game or clear its storage, it is removed.

Online weekly leaderboard

To show weekly ranks and give out the gold, silver and bronze chests, the game uses Unity Gaming Services (Authentication and Leaderboards), run by Unity Technologies:

  • Anonymous player ID. When you are online, the game signs in anonymously to Unity. Unity creates a random player ID so your score can be saved. It is not linked to your name, email, phone number or Google account.
  • Runner name. The game picks a random runner name for you (for example a made-up animal name). You can only change it to another random name. You cannot type your own name, so no personal information is shown to other players.
  • Scores. Your best run scores for each week are sent to the leaderboard and shown to other players next to your runner name.
  • Like any internet service, Unity's servers can see your device's IP address when the game connects. See the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

This data is used only to run the leaderboard. It is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). If you play offline, nothing is sent.

Purchases

In-app purchases (gems, coins, the Starter Pack and the World Pass) are processed by Google Play. We do not see or store your card or payment details. Google Play tells the game only whether a purchase was successful. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Ads, analytics and tracking

Shift Runner does not show ads, does not use the Android Advertising ID, and does not use analytics or tracking tools. It does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Sharing your data

We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone. The only service that receives data is Unity Gaming Services, which runs the leaderboard for us as explained above.

Deleting your data

  • On your phone: clear the game's storage in Android Settings > Apps > Shift Runner > Storage, or uninstall the game. This removes all of your progress saved on the phone.
  • Leaderboard data: email us at littleorderstudio@gmail.com with the subject "Shift Runner data deletion" and include your runner name as shown on the Weekly Ranks screen. We will delete your anonymous player ID, runner name and scores from Unity Gaming Services within 30 days and reply to confirm. Nothing is kept after deletion.

Children

Shift Runner is made for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you think a child has used the leaderboard, email us and we will delete that data.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes how it uses data (for example if ads are added in the future), we will update this page and the date at the top before the change is released.
